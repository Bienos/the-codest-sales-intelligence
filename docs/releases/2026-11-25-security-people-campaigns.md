# 25 Nov 2026: security fixes, people database, signal campaigns, more sources

## What changed for sellers
- **Today** opens with *Buying signals right now*: every kind of signal that is live, in plain words ("Funding round", "Payments scheme change"), how many companies show it, what it means for us, and *Start a campaign*. Picking a tile filters the findings below it. Every finding now names its signal and says why it is a buying signal.
- **People** (new): one list of everyone you can contact from the workbooks, Apollo, Pipedrive and Lemlist, with the company's current signal, who owns the account, email status and what happened in outreach. Addresses are never shown.
- **Campaigns** (new): pick one or more signals; the platform builds the audience (every company in your scope with that signal live and verified, up to five people each with a verified email, nobody owned by another seller, opted out or at a company with an open deal) and explains every exclusion. Each email opens with that company's own verified news. The copy is brand-checked for every lead. A manager approves the exact list and copy; *Push to Lemlist* creates the campaign and leads but never launches it.
- **Accounts** show the Pipedrive owner, how many people have a verified email, a plain signal name with why it matters, and a campaign link.
- **Search** ignores accents and case ("krakow" finds "Kraków") and matches short words whole ("cto" no longer matches "director").
- **Sign-in**: visible Sign in button, the company domain shown on the card, full-width *Continue with Google*, no blank logo while it loads.

## Security fixes (all with regression tests in `backend/tests/test_security_hardening.py`)
| Severity | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| High | DNS rebinding could make the verifier fetch internal addresses; any port was allowed | Connections are pinned to the checked public address; ports 80/443 only |
| High | Admin *Test source* followed redirects without the public-address check and echoed remote bodies | Uses the protected client; only our own message is shown |
| Medium | Any seller could approve or run approvals for accounts outside their scope | Decide/execute need visibility of the account; bulk campaigns need a manager |
| Medium | Search and the feed listed claims on other sellers' accounts | Row scope applied |
| Medium | *Add company* on an existing out-of-scope domain attached notes and queued work | Refused without revealing the owner |
| Medium | Late opportunity stages accepted made-up CRM references | Manager only; references must exist in the Pipedrive mirror |
| Low | People from other companies could be put on a card; sessions renewed forever; `email_verified` missing was accepted; dev tokens allowed if `RI_ENV` had a typo; KB links signed with the database address; webhook 500s on odd input; no rate limit | All fixed (see commit) |

**Found while testing:** the findings archive failed for every seller (a query dropped its scope parameters). Fixed.

## To switch things on (Render)

|---|---|
