How to read this

Method

Each simulated year plays out every open deal and every deal lemlist and the webform are expected to create from December 2026 on. A deal either advances or drops at each stage, and each stage takes a random time around its median. A deal that reaches the end earns its value in the quarter it closes.

Bars show the average across 2,000 simulated years. The whisker is the P10 to P90 range: 8 in 10 simulated years land inside it. The median and the chance of hitting target come from the same runs.

Every conversion, duration and deal count below is a placeholder. Replace them with measured values once deal stage history is captured. Until a stage has about 20 to 30 real deals, treat its rate as a guess and read the range, not the median.

Assumptions

Stage names come from the Pipedrive stage map in the repo. Confirm whether DISCOVERY sits before or after SQL and edit the names to match. Marketing Nurturing is left out on purpose: treat it as a parking stage, not a step.

Sources: lemlist campaign statistics read through the lemlist API on 27 Nov 2026. Pipedrive stage names from backend/src/ri/deal/progress.py. Funnel, reply and cycle benchmarks, and the case for these metrics, are in docs/sales-metrics-and-forecast.md.

Not modelled yet: stage-to-stage differences by source or country, tenders, deal-value spread by service line, revenue recognised over time, and Marketing Nurturing re-entry. Lost deals are assumed to leave at the end of the stage they fail in.
